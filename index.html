How the numbers work

Hit rate: share of games clearing the line (pushes excluded).
Est. prob: weighted blend of last 5/10/20 and season hit rates, shrunk toward 50% for small samples.
Implied: probability from the odds, including the book's cut.
Edge: est. prob minus implied. It only looks at past results: no matchups or injuries. With live data it also nudges the estimate when recent minutes differ from normal (up to 8 points).
Parlays assume independent legs.

Reading a play card

OVER / UNDER 18.5 Points: the pick. Over means the player finishes above the line, under means below. The line always ends in .5, so there are no ties.
CLE vs ATL · NBA · -119: his team vs tonight's opponent, then the sportsbook odds for that side (American odds).
Colored badge: the edge. +8.2% green is 5% or more, +2.0% amber is above 0, -3.0% red is negative.
L5 / L10 / L20 / Season: how often he cleared this line in his last 5, 10 and 20 games, and across all games loaded. Live data includes last season too.
Est vs implied: our estimated chance of hitting vs the chance the odds imply. The gap is the edge.
Streak: games in a row he has cleared the line, counting back from his latest game.
+ Parlay / Details: add the play to your parlay, or open the Player tab for a game-by-game chart.

The numbers

Est. prob: a blend of his hit rates (last 5 counts most, then 10, 20 and season), pulled toward 50% when there are few games.
Implied: the chance built into the odds. It includes the sportsbook's cut, so over and under don't add to 100%.
Edge: est. prob minus implied. Positive means we think the bet is priced too low.
EV / unit: expected profit per 1 unit staked, if our estimate is right.
Trend: his last-5 average vs his normal, in standard deviations. Positive means running hot.

Picks tab

Best value: the 3 or 4 plays with the biggest edge. Safest: the plays most likely to hit, even if the edge is smaller.
Each slip has one leg per player and per game. Plays are skipped if the player has an injury flag, the game has blowout risk, his minutes are dropping, or the game has already started.
Pays / win / EV: the payout multiple, our estimated chance every leg hits, and expected profit per unit. A 4-leg slip hits rarely, so treat these as ideas, not locks.
Load into Parlay: copies the slip to the Parlay tab so you can set a stake.

Parlays

Payout multiple: what a winning parlay returns per 1 staked, including the stake.
Est. win prob: our chances of all legs hitting, treating legs as unrelated. Legs from the same game often move together, so treat this as a rough guide.

Where the data comes from

Lines and odds: DraftKings via ESPN. Game logs and minutes: ESPN. Injuries: Sleeper. All free and unofficial, so they can be late or wrong, and a late scratch may not show up yet.
The over and under odds are matched by order from ESPN and haven't been verified against DraftKings.
NFL: ESPN lists DraftKings lines for NFL but no prices, so every NFL play is shown at an assumed -110 (marked *). Edge, EV and parlay payouts for NFL are rough, and NFL plays are left out of the Picks tab. NFL injuries come from ESPN, and minutes, back-to-back and opponent-defense lines do not apply.
The model only looks at past results and minutes. It does not know matchups, pace, or teammates' injuries.
